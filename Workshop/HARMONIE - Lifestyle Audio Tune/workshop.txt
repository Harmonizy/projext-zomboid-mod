title=HARMONIE - Lifestyle Audio Tune
description=Turns down "Lifestyle: Hobbies" (Workshop ID 3403870858) music and sound effects -- they ship very loud and can be heard from way too far away.
description=
description=Cuts the disco/jukebox/DJ booth music, playable instruments (guitar, harmonica, etc.) and other equipment sounds (button presses, turning things on/off) to half volume, with a shared 25-tile max hearing range (down from the original 75-100 tiles). The Jukebox's actual song already has its own right-click "Jukebox Volume" menu in Lifestyle itself, so that one's left alone.
description=
description=Also adds a real settings page (Options -> Mods -> "HARMONIE: Lifestyle Audio Tune") with a live volume slider -- changes apply instantly, no restart needed. Hearing range isn't live-adjustable (the game has no API for that), so it stays fixed at 25 tiles.
description=
description=Also wires up 4 JoJo's Bizarre Adventure openings (Sono Chi no Sadame, Bloody Stream, Fighting Gold, Giorno's Theme), the "HEYYEYAAEYAAAEYAEYAA" meme song, and three more requested tracks (Bothnia - Someday I'll Wait, Violette Wautier - Wanna Be Yours, Honkai: Star Rail - What the Ripple Sees) as playable instrument tracks -- each song shows up on every real instrument that fits it (Electric Guitar, Bass, Acoustic Guitar, Saxophone, Trumpet, Keytar, Harmonica, Flute, Violin). Audio files are NOT included -- they're copyrighted; drop your own legally-obtained .ogg files into media/sound/ yourself (see the README there for exact filenames).
description=
description=Requires Lifestyle: Hobbies to be enabled, and must load after it (enforced automatically via require=).
description=
