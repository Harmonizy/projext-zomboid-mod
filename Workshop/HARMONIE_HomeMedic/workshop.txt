title=HARMONIE - Home Medic
description=A complete health overhaul for Build 42, all in one mod. Extensive Health Rework Evolved B42 (Workshop 3726328119) and The Only Cure (Workshop 3580276809) are merged in with their authors' permission: blood volume, diseases, wounds and infections, medication, stitching, surgery, amputation and prostheses -- one mod, one health window, one settings page.
description=
description=HARMONIE ADDITIONS
description=- Traditional/folk medicine items made from herbs, everyday materials and even vanilla insects.
description=- Blood loss you can feel: your stamina ceiling drops with blood volume, the screen edges darken, and below the moderate threshold the view dims and throbs with your heartbeat (vision can be turned down or off in Options -> Mods).
description=- The Only Cure surgery options (amputate, cauterize, prosthesis, clean the stump) inside the medical window, also when treating another player, and the limb state (amputated, healing %, stump dirtiness, prosthesis) shown there too.
description=- SURGERY: 10 operations from the medical window's Surgery tab -- Debridement, Abscess Drainage, Foreign Body Removal, Parasite Extraction, Thoracic, Organ Salvage, Neurosurgery, Blood Purification, Amputation (The Only Cure) and Experimental Knox gene therapy -- built from 15 procedure minigames (incision, hemostasis, irrigation, necrectomy, aspiration, extraction, bone saw, closure, drainage valve, catheter, dialysis, cell graft, craniotomy drill, clot evacuation, organ repair).
description=- The procedures look and sound like surgery: a draped operative field with Betadine-prepped skin, real tissue (muscle, fat, organ, necrosis), blood that spurts and pools, the actual instrument in your hand (scalpel, clamp, needle holder, forceps, syringe, suction, bone saw, drill, pipette) and a patient monitor whose heart rate climbs and alarms when you hurt the patient. All art and sounds are made for this mod.
description=- A pre-op checklist (indication, skill, instruments in your bags / on the floor / in nearby containers, asepsis, anesthesia, blood) with tooltips. Procedures unlock with First Aid and disease knowledge from flyers.
description=- A successful operation puts the disease on treatment. Advanced stages of serious diseases need surgery: medicine alone only halts them ("awaiting surgery").
description=- DIAGNOSIS: every illness shows as "Unknown" until someone diagnoses it. Tick the signs the patient shows (fever, cough, vomiting...) and the list narrows to the illnesses that cause all of them; only illnesses you know (disease flyer or First Aid 8) can be picked. Then the window names it and shows what it is doing to the body. Can be switched off in Sandbox.
description=- One medical window, seven tabs with our own icons: Monitor, Immune System, Body Stats (every stat of you or your patient, including stats other mods add such as From Garden to Plate's vitamins), Diagnosis, Surgery (every operation; pick one the patient needs, check the pre-op list, Start opens the operating window), Disease Handbook (J) and Medication Handbook (every medicine with its picture, what it cures or only eases, dosing and side effects). Both handbooks can be searched and filtered. The vanilla health window still opens as usual; the medical window opens with it. Pin / unpin it like the inventory; the Medical Monitor always opens expanded.
description=- The disease handbook is built from the game's own data: the real symptoms of every stage, HOW TO CHECK each sign (which reading in Body Stats, or what to watch for), and which medicines cure it or only ease the symptoms. It also explains the surgery for each disease.
description=- Systematic diagnosis: the Monitor tab shows a live pulse trace (good / fair / poor / critical / no pulse, with the reasons). Body Stats needs a powered medical monitor watch on the patient and shows the blood composition, the vanilla moodles and every sign it can measure right now (fever from body temperature, headache from head pain...).
description=- Diagnose first: an illness can only be operated on once it is diagnosed. Picking a diagnosis plays a short moment of suspense, then the verdict with shortcuts to the handbook or the operation. Signs measured on the patient right now are marked on the diagnosis chips, and every measurable sign follows what the illness really does in game.
description=- Surgery uses blood bags and saline: when an operation would leave the patient low on blood, a compatible blood bag (or saline) must be at hand; the wrong blood type causes a transfusion reaction. Instruments accept more everyday substitutes, and missing items are named as in your game. Every operation has its own picture.
description=- One blue theme for every window of the mod. An amputated limb turns black on the body diagram. Fit or remove a prosthesis by right-clicking the stump. Illness rows show "Undiagnosed" (you know it, diagnose it) or "Unknown" (you do not know it), with Diagnose / Surgery buttons.
description=- Surgery: a pre-op "Fasting (NPO)" check -- operating right after a meal risks aspiration pneumonia, more so when the patient is asleep or sedated. Chest, abdomen, skull, dialysis and gene therapy cannot be done on yourself (allowed in a -debug game or for server admins).
description=- Delirium sound volume and mute, per player. Only your own machine is affected; visions, lines and impulses are unchanged.
description=- One Options -> Mods page (keys, health-panel switches, delirium sound, blood vision) and one set of Sandbox pages ("Home Medic: ...").
description=- English and Thai built in -- every line the character says and every label in the medical window is translated. The Thai lines are written as people really talk: grumbling, swearing and all.
description=- Body Stats shows abnormal values in red (fever, low blood, pain, panic...), so trouble stands out at a glance.
description=- A cured illness caught again must be diagnosed again. A medicine course pops up a reminder when the next dose is due. After an operation the patient is left weak: 70% blood at most, less after a messy one.
description=- Caffeine pills are their own item now; vanilla's vitamin pills are vitamins again (and work with our From Garden to Plate mod as multivitamins).
description=- Translation keys keep the original Extensive Health Rework / The Only Cure names, so other mods that use them keep working. EHR debug menu always available in a -debug game or to server admins.
description=- Lighter on dedicated servers (per-tick work trimmed); the medical window fits 720p screens; blood-loss vision no longer darkens your windows.
description=
description=The old stitching minigame and the folk-medicine recipes are switched off for now.
description=
description=IMPORTANT: this mod REPLACES Extensive Health Rework Evolved and The Only Cure. Do NOT enable either of them together with Home Medic. Existing saves keep their health and amputation data (the same data keys are used).
