Scale everything together from 0-5x (default 1.0 = unchanged), without touching vanilla loot or the mod's own weapon attachments/parts.

description=(2) Guards against a rare bug where reloading could fail to actually use up ammo, caused by a race in the base mod's own magazine-refresh code.

description=
description=(4) Fills in roughly 90 more English translation gaps across item names, recipes, tooltips and UI labels -- without these, players saw raw Chinese text on about 60 guns/ammo/parts and several recipes, since the game falls back to the item's literal internal name when no translation exists.
