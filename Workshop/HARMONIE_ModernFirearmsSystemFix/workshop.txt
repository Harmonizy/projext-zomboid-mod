description=
description=(2) A defensive guard against a possible ammo-not-depleting race in the mod's own magazine-refresh system (ChangeMagazineType.lua), which can swap the held weapon for a fresh item mid-action; the guard defers that resync while the player has a timed action queued.
description=
description=(3) Supplies the missing English text for the mod's own in-game Mod Options page (Options -> Mods -> Modern Firearms System) -- it only ever shipped Chinese translations for those strings.
description=
description=(4) Fills in ~90 more English translation gaps across item names, recipes, tooltips and UI labels the mod only ever shipped in Chinese -- players in English (and every other non-Chinese language) were seeing raw Chinese text on ~60 items (guns, ammo reloading schematics, weapon parts) and several recipes/UI labels, since Project Zomboid falls back to the item script's literal DisplayName field when no translation key matches.
description=
