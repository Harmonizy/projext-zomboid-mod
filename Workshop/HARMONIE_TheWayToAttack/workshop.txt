title=HARMONIE - The Way To Attack (WIP)
description=(WIP) A melee weapon-crafting mod for Build 42: 137 HARMONIE weapons plus every vanilla melee weapon with a real Weaponry recipe, all crafted through a custom procedural crafting window.
description=
description=HOW IT WORKS
description=- Pick a recipe and press Start: the base material and the supplementary material are taken right away, and the window stays on that recipe until you Cancel, stop it (Incomplete) or Finish.
description=- Every recipe needs a set of procedures (sharpen, forge, quench, weld, wrap, rivet, drill, sew, engrave...). Each one is played as a short skill-based MINIGAME and scored Miss / Bad / Good / Excellent. A Miss must be redone; any procedure can be redone to improve it.
description=- Finish averages the procedure scores into an overall quality, and the weapon's grade (S to F) is rolled from that quality's pool.
description=- Stop half way and you get the item itself, UNFINISHED (0 damage) with the progress saved on it: right-click it later - you or another player - to carry on.
description=- A metallurgy chain of crafted materials (Material Lump, Tin / Titanium / Mithril / Adamantium / Vibranium bars; the top three also need Oridecon / Elunium / Bradium). Materials need Good or Excellent overall quality to finish.
description=- Forges matter: higher procedures need a better forge nearby, and each forging level cools faster.
description=- Multiplayer-safe: every world change happens server-side.
description=- Its own working sounds for every procedure and minigame (volume in Options > Mods).
description=- Uses tools and materials from containers and the floor around you, and a Practice button for every minigame.
description=- A grade reveal show on the anvil when a weapon is finished.
description=- 17 gemstones and a gem-cutting skill tree (selection, analysis, planning, sawing, preforming, faceting, polishing, fine cutting, quality control), each with its own minigame.
description=- Admin / -debug weapon stat editor: right-click any weapon to edit every stat it has (multiplayer-safe, and the edit really counts in combat).
description=- Detailed sandbox settings (8 pages): minigames on/off and sounds, action times, XP, light/forge rules, quality cut points, grade chances, and every zone, speed and time of every minigame.
description=
description=Requires "NeatUI Framework" (Workshop ID 3508537032) - please subscribe to it too, and make sure it loads before this mod.
