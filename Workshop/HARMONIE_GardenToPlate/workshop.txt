title=HARMONIE - From Garden to Plate
description=Adds a real vitamin system (A, B, C, D, E, K) on top of the food you already eat. Just about every vanilla food feeds into one or more vitamins, so eating a varied diet actually matters now, not just calories.
description=
description=Check your own or a nearby survivor's status anytime with N (rebindable), or just open your character info panel -- there's a new "Vitamins" tab that shows everyone's status at a glance, exact numbers once you hit First Aid 3, and a full "what's rich in this vitamin" food guide at Cooking 3 (or if your character has the Nutritionist trait).
description=
description=Let a vitamin run out and you'll actually feel it -- each one has its own real, distinct symptom instead of a generic debuff: A gives you blurred vision, B brings on numbness/headaches/stress, C causes random nosebleeds, D leaves your muscles aching and stiff, E makes you persistently unhappy and slow to react, and K just wears your whole body down. Let several run out at once and your stamina takes an extra hit on top. Eating well banks "pause days" that hold the symptoms off for a while, so staying ahead of your diet is what protects you -- not scrambling once you're already sick.
description=
description=Also adds home canning (preserve veggies and fruit indefinitely in empty tin cans, no vitamins lost) and a craftable multivitamin pill for emergencies.
description=
description=Works great alongside Moodle Framework (per-vitamin warning moodles) and Modern Status (optional HUD bars) if you have them -- both required.
description=
description=Singleplayer-focused; see the mod's README for the full mechanics writeup.
