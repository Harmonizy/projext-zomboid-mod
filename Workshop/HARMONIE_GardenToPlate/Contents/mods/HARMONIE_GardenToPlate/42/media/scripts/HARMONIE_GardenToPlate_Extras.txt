/*
    HARMONIE - From Garden to Plate: foods that keep vitamins through the
    winter (2026-10-08, from the suggestions list).
      Dried<Fruit>   one fresh fruit dried on a drying rack (the same rack
                     recipe shape as Fruit Farming's rice / coffee, Tags =
                     DryingRackGrain) -- keeps for months; vitamin C partly
                     lost in drying (HARMONIE_ExtraFoodVitamins.lua)
      BeanSprouts    dried beans sprouted in a jar -- vitamin C and K from a
                     pantry, any season
      FishLiverOil   rendered from fish in a pot -- a lot of vitamin A and D
    Icons are this mod's own (tools/gen_food_icons.py).
*/

    /* Bean sprouts (0.13.0, checked against vanilla B42's own scripts):
       1. soak: a jar with water (vanilla's own "-fluid ... categories[Water]",
          like PlaceRiceInCookingPot) and 10 points of dried beans -- the
          bag is only partly used, the way vanilla's rice pot uses a bag
          of rice (a whole 60-point bag used to become three sprouts).
       2. wait: the soaking jar turns into a jar of sprouts by itself after
          about 2-3 days (vanilla's own ReplaceOnRotten, the same thing
          that turns Sugar Beet Syrup into sugar). A fridge slows it.
       3. empty the jar: 4 handfuls of sprouts and the jar back. */
