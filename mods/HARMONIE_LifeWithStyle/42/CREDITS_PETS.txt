HARMONIE - Life With Style: pet credits
=======================================

The pets' dog models, body textures, portraits (our icons) and voices come
from the mod "Companion Dogs" (Steam Workshop 3740052292), used in this mod
with its author's permission (owner of HARMONIE, 2026-10-23). Thank you!

Taken (renamed LWSPet_*, nothing else of it is included):
  models_X/Skinned/LWSPet_<Breed>.glb  <- Companion Dogs' <Breed>_Body.glb
                                          (its own 3D models, rigged to the
                                          vanilla raccoon skeleton)
  textures/Body/LWSPet_<Breed>.png     <- its body textures
  textures/Item_LWSPet_<Breed>.png     <- its CDPortrait_*.png, made smaller
  AnimSets/raccoon/*/lws*.xml          <- its animation nodes (walk, run, idle
                                          variations, lying down, sniffing,
                                          eating), renamed; its attack nodes
                                          are not used
  sound/LWSPet_*.ogg                   <- its dog voices. Companion Dogs credits
                                          them as CC0: BigSoundBank.com (Joseph
                                          Sardin) for the barks, whines and
                                          huffs; Freesound.org "a dog eats his
                                          kibble" by aisselles (#725619) for
                                          eating.

The cats come from "CD: Cats" (Steam Workshop 3791294616), the Companion Dogs
add-on by the same author, used with permission as well:
  models_X/Skinned/LWSPet_Cat.glb      <- its Cat_Body.glb. Its credits say the
                                          model was rebuilt from a third-party
                                          GLB, and that the lie-down, sitting
                                          and limping animation sets inside it
                                          were authored by Cerise
                                          (steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198351533061)
  textures/Body/LWSPet_Cat_<Coat>.png  <- its coats; BrownTabby, GreyTabby,
                                          OrangeTabby, Barley, Cow and Tortie
                                          were painted by Cerise
  textures/Item_LWSPet_Cat_<Coat>.png  <- its CDPortrait_cat.png; the coats
                                          other than black are our recolour of it
  AnimSets/raccoon/idle/lwsSit*.xml    <- its sitting nodes, renamed
  sound/LWSPet_Cat*.ogg                <- its cat voices: BigSoundBank.com
                                          (Joseph Sardin), "free and royalty free"

The code that makes the pets follow, take orders and eat is our own
(lua/server/HARMONIELifeWithStyle/LWS_Pets.lua); none of Companion Dogs' code
is included. tools/import_pets.py copies the files above.
