HARMONIE - Modern Firearms -- credits
=====================================

This mod is based on "Modern Firearms System" (Steam Workshop item
3633421539, mod id ModernFirearmsSystem), whose author is no longer
developing it. Its guns, ammunition, magazines, weapon parts and
attachments, 3D models, textures, animations, sounds, item scripts and its
3D inspect / modification interface are the original author's work, used
and redistributed here WITH THE ORIGINAL AUTHOR'S PERMISSION (obtained by
HARMONIE, 2026-10-09). All credit for that work goes to its author.

Taken over (gun-related parts only): guns, ammo, magazines, parts and
attachments, the inspect / 3D view, reload / eject / fire systems,
grenades and launchers, the flamethrower, shields -- with their models,
textures, animations and sounds, and the original's translations.
Left out: the air drops and the radio trade.

HARMONIE's own additions: the gun / ammo drop-rate sandbox multiplier,
the ammo-not-depleting guard, Inspect only on firearms, English (and
Thai) texts the original never shipped, and the HARMONIE window work.

Do not load this mod together with the original Modern Firearms System:
both define the same items (on purpose, so guns in existing saves keep
working).
